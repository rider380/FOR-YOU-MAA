A festive wish · with all my love

Happy Dasara,
my Alekhya Pichidi ✨

Naa bangaram Alekhya… ee Dasara nee jeevitham lo santosham, manashanthi, manchi aarogyam, andamaina kalalu nindali. Nee navvu eppudu ilaane veluguthu undali; nee adugulu anni manchi daari lo munduku vellali. Chedu meeda manchi gelichinattu, nee life lo unna prathi badha ni dhairyam tho gelichi, nee kalalanni nijam chesukovali.

Nuvvu naaku special ani cheppadaniki oka festival avasaram ledu… kaani ee Dasara roju, naa manasulo neeku unna place entha precious-o marosari cheppalanipinchindi. Nee navvu naaku oka pandaga, nee happiness naaku oka blessing, nee presence naa life lo oka beautiful feeling.

Dasara Subhakankshalu, naa bangaram ❤️
